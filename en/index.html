Context

Why Namibia — Right now

The world has entered a phase of turbulence: geopolitical shifts, currency instability, unpredictable rule changes in familiar jurisdictions. Against this backdrop, demand is growing for quiet, stable places — not for escape, but for sober risk distribution.

Namibia is a rare example of a country that has stayed outside the focus of conflict for years, while opening real, lawful paths to residency and capital protection. This isn't an offshore scheme or a grey zone — it's a full jurisdiction with clear rules, a working economy, and growing interest from private investors worldwide.

02 · A quiet harbor for capital

Capital loves quiet more than yield

Namibia offers exactly that combination — a jurisdiction where the rules don't change with the regulator's mood.

Banking: loyalty, not suspicion

Unlike EU banks, where compliance blocks accounts for "too foreign" clients for years, Namibian banks open accounts for foreigners and businesses through a clear KYC procedure — no months of waiting, no presumption of guilt.

EU

Months of checks, refusals for "complicated" clients, frozen accounts

Namibia

Transparent requirements, real access to service

Currency stability

The Namibian dollar is pegged 1:1 to the South African rand within the Common Monetary Area — no sudden devaluations "by one person's decision."

Equal rights

Full freehold property ownership, including land (except agricultural land). A foreigner buys it on the same terms as a citizen.

100% foreign ownership

No local partner is required (except in a few sectors), and a local director isn't required in most industries either.

A clear tax system

The same tax, duty, and licensing rates apply to everyone — not a "special regime for foreigners" that can be revoked at any moment.

What "quiet harbor" means in practice

  • No practice of sudden nationalization or targeted confiscation of foreigners' assets
  • No currency controls blocking the repatriation of income from a real business
  • Courts and registration bodies operate under an established, predictable procedure
  • Resident status and business ownership are legal and transparent, not a "grey" construct

The Orange Basin oil frontier

Since 2022, TotalEnergies, Shell, Galp, and QatarEnergy have made a series of major discoveries off Namibia's coast. The Venus project is moving toward a final investment decision in 2026 — a potential comparable to the Guyana boom.

A bet on "green" hydrogen

The flagship Hyphen project — $10B in investment, with its first 2 GW phase launching as early as 2026, built on the region's best solar and wind resources.

Additional growth drivers

  • Uranium and gold mining — the main growth driver in 2024–2025
  • Walvis Bay port modernization — over $2.1B invested
  • Talks on nuclear-energy cooperation
  • Growth in tourism, logistics, and the financial sector
Namibia is increasingly called "the next Guyana" — a country where a fortunate geographic coincidence meets disciplined state policy. The difference from the Persian Gulf is fundamental: Namibia is a secular, pluralistic republic with no sharia law or religious restrictions on business.
04 · Built for calm

Calm in uncalm times

Namibia is one of the world's least densely populated countries — about 3 people per square kilometer. Space becomes a psychological resource — distance from noise, rush, and other people's anxiety.

Less noise→clearer decisions→safer capital→a calmer life

What this gives you first

  • A way to step back from information and social overload
  • A legal, understandable "plan B" — not an escape, but a conscious choice
  • An unhurried pace of business: less bureaucratic rush
  • Room for family and long-term decisions, without rushing

The path to residency — in short

The most stable route is residency through a small business (business-led residency): company registration, a lease, real operating activity, a work and residence permit for 2 years with renewal. The law sets no formal minimum investment.

Residency

The path to residency: RP and PR

Two statuses — two planning horizons. A temporary Residence Permit gives you a legal foundation to start; indefinite Permanent Residence gives you long-term certainty. We support you at every stage: from the first filing to permanent status.

RESIDENCE PERMIT

RP — Residence Permit

From €3500 to €5000 — depending on the type of activity and the term of the permit (1 to 5 years).

  • Live and work in Namibia legally
  • Open accounts at Namibian banks
  • Register and run a business
  • Buy property on equal terms with citizens
  • Extend status to a spouse and children
  • Enter and leave the country freely
PERMANENT RESIDENCE

PR — Permanent Residence

Granted after 5 years of continuous Residence Permit status, issued indefinitely. It can also be obtained immediately, based on investment in the Namibian economy with mandatory job creation.

  • Indefinite status — no need to renew
  • A direct route through job-creating investment
  • A foundation for business and family for generations
  • Unrestricted entry and exit
⚠

Other routes to Permanent Residence are discussed at the initial consultation.

Legal status isn't a formality — it's a foundation: for business, for family, for peace of mind for years to come.
